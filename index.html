How it began

Our Love Story

Seven years, two cities and one very persistent coffee order.

  1. 2019

    A misdelivered coffee

    Annushka got Ayush's oat-milk latte by mistake in a Delhi café. She kept it. He asked for her number instead.

  2. 2020

    Long-distance & longer calls

    Two cities, one shared playlist, and 3 AM video calls that neither of them ever hung up first.

  3. 2022

    Meeting the families

    Two chaotic Sunday lunches later, both mothers were already exchanging recipes and wedding ideas.

  4. 2024

    The Ghats Proposal

    A boat ride along the Ganga ghats at twilight, a hidden ring, and an overjoyed yes.

  5. 2026

    Forever, officially

    Everything we love in one place — our people, our city of lakes, and each other.

Getting here

Travel & Stay

Flights, trains, room blocks and the little things nobody tells you.

By Air

Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS), Varanasi is 40 minutes from Ramada. Shuttles will be scheduled on 18–19 November.

By Train

Varanasi City Junction is 8 km away. Pre-booked cabs will greet guests with a #AyushWedsAnnushka placard.

Where to Stay

Room blocks are held at The Leela Palace, Trident Varanasi and Hotel Lakend. Use code VR2026 for guest rates.

Good to Know

November evenings in Varanasi are pleasant (16°C). Morning boat rides along the ghats can be arranged at the hospitality desk.
